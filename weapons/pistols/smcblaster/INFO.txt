Name: Space Marine Corps Blaster
Class: 2
Type: Projectile
Summon: SMCBlaster
Ammo Type: None
Altfire: No
Palette: Doom
Brightmaps: Yes

Description:
This is the standard issue rechargeable energy side-arm. It does not require ammunition.
Useful for taking down Imps as well as exploding barrels and setting off shootable buttons and secret doors.
Keep one on you at all times.

Note: to replace the standard Doom pistol with the SMC Blaster, uncomment the PlayerClasses line in the GameInfo block in MAPINFO.txt.
